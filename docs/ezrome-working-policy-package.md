## 3. Privacy Policy — working draft

**Effective date:** [OWNER TO COMPLETE]

EZROME is operated by **Sixolile Ezrome Mtyhali / EZROME** in South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how EZROME handles personal information when users visit the service, create an account, publish content, interact with community features, use limited offline playback, report material, or contact support.

### Information EZROME may process

EZROME may process account identity supplied through its authentication flow; creator profile information; media files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and download settings supplied by creators; posts and other community submissions; content reports; deletion requests; offline-library records stored on the user’s device; technical and usage information needed to operate the service; and support communications.

### Why EZROME processes information

EZROME uses this information to authenticate users, provide creator stations and media playback, operate community and moderation features, provide rights-controlled offline access, respond to reports and deletion requests, secure and improve the service, and prepare AI-assisted drafts subject to human approval. EZROME does not represent AI-generated newsroom material as final editorial publication without human approval.

### Storage and sharing

Creator media is stored through managed storage. Account, content, reporting, and entitlement records are stored in the application database. The final policy must identify the production service providers, analytics providers, notification providers, countries of processing, and any circumstances in which information is shared or disclosed. **[OWNER TO COMPLETE after production vendor review]**

### Retention and deletion

EZROME provides an in-app deletion request flow and a public web deletion resource. The publisher must define the timing for processing deletion requests, the categories of information deleted, and any records retained for security, legal, tax, dispute, copyright, or fraud-prevention purposes. **[OWNER TO COMPLETE]**

### Rights and contact

## 5. Community Guidelines — V1 draft

EZROME exists for original culture, hip-hop, entertainment, football intelligence, creator work, and constructive community conversation. Users should share work they have the right to share, credit creators where appropriate, and protect the privacy and dignity of others.

Do not use EZROME for copyright infringement, unlawful material, non-consensual sexual content, exploitation, credible threats, targeted harassment, hateful abuse, doxxing, deceptive impersonation, malicious spam, or material that violates applicable law. Drug references, profanity, political discussion, and mature cultural commentary are not automatically prohibited, but they must remain lawful, contextual, non-exploitative, and consistent with the final platform and store policies.

Users may report material for copyright, harassment, sexual safety, drug-related risk, violence, privacy, spam, or another concern. Reports enter a human review queue. The final procedure must document response targets, escalation, appeal, correction, evidence retention, and emergency removal. **[OWNER TO COMPLETE]**
